# AirBand Privacy Policy

Effective September 5, 2026

AirBand uses the iPhone camera to detect hand positions and facial expressions for real-time musical control. This processing happens on the device. AirBand does not record, save, transmit, or share camera images, face data, hand data, audio, identifiers, analytics, or personal information.

AirBand does not contain advertising, third-party analytics, accounts, or tracking technologies.

Camera access can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings. Without camera permission, the app remains playable in Touch mode.

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the support contact listed on AirBand's App Store product page.
